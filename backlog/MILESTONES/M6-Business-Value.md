## Business Case

### AI-Readiness Framework

**Problem:** PE firms struggle to identify which portfolio companies can successfully adopt AI and which cannot.

**Solution:** Solstein assesses AI readiness across 5 dimensions: data maturity, technical infrastructure, talent, use case clarity, and executive alignment.

**Value:** PE firms can prioritize AI investments, avoid failed transformations, and accelerate value creation.

### Energy Sector Specialization

**Problem:** Energy trading companies need specialized intelligence on compliance, forecasting, and grid integration.

**Solution:** Solstein provides energy-specific scoring models, regulatory tracking, and market forecasting.

**Value:** Energy firms get actionable intelligence tailored to their unique challenges.
