When a Dino becomes a Riser, that equity multiplies. When a Riser becomes a Rocket, you're sitting on a portfolio of companies you picked because you *knew* they were undervalued, and you *made* them valuable.

That's not consulting. That's not SaaS. That's a value creation engine.

## AI Sharks -- A Signal, Not an Army

A new species: **AI Sharks**. Small, agile AI teams. Fast, hungry. Tool chains set up.

Two stages:
- **Cooking:** Spitting out products fast. Quantity over quality.
- **Maturing:** Good-enough-ish products. Finding fit.

What they have: speed, no legacy. What they lack: **the brain.** Market knowledge, domain depth, architectural judgment.

### Sharks Are Not Our Play

Too immature. Quality risk too high.

### Sharks Are a Signal on the Radar

| Signal | What It Means | Who Cares |
|---|---|---|
| Sharks cooking in a market | New AI-native entrants disrupting | PE firms with Dinos in that market |
| Sharks maturing | Disruption window narrowing | PE firms who need to act NOW |
| Novices looking for a guild | Promising young teams with skill but no direction | AI-Whishperers -- potential recruits |

### The Real Target: The Leaded Brain

The shark has speed but no brain. The Dino has the brain but it's **leaded** -- chained by legacy.

**AI-Whishperers' play:** Find the leaded brains, remove the shackles, add AI. The result: someone with 10-20 years domain expertise AND AI-native speed AND architectural judgment. An unchained brain with AI tooling **destroys** a shark.
