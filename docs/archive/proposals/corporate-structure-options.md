## Three Structural Options

### Option A: AI-Whishperers Owns Everything, Eneve Gets Commercial Partnership

- AI-Whishperers owns 100% of Solstein IP and SaaS platform
- John contributes IP via license or assignment agreement to AI-Whishperers
- Eneve/Michiel gets a commercial partnership agreement (not equity)
- Gaston gets advisory role

**When this works:** When the family wants to keep full control and treat Eneve as a sales channel.

### Option B: Solstein as Joint Venture (AI-Whishperers + Eneve)

- New entity: Solstein BV
- Equity split between AI-Whishperers, Eneve, and Gaston
- John contributes IP + ongoing development
- Michiel contributes sales + commercial operations

**When this works:** When both parties want a long-term committed partnership and Michiel's PE network access is genuinely irreplaceable.

### Option C: AI-Whishperers Owns Solstein, Licenses to "Solstein Commercial BV" (Recommended)

- AI-Whishperers owns 100% of the Solstein IP, codebase, and SaaS platform
- A separate commercial entity (Solstein Commercial BV) is created
- Solstein Commercial BV licenses the platform from AI-Whishperers to sell to PE clients
- License fee structure ensures AI-Whishperers gets paid regardless
- Michiel runs commercial operations
- Gaston gets founding stake
- AI-Whishperers retains unrestricted access for its own transformation business

**When this works:** When the family wants to give Michiel a real business to run while keeping the crown jewels (IP + platform) safely in AI-Whishperers.

| **Future flexibility** | Can sell Commercial BV, license to others, or bring in new partners |

## Key Terms to Define

1. **IP ownership** -- Who owns the codebase, prompts, templars, exemplars, scoring algorithms?
2. **License scope** -- Exclusive to PE? What about corporate strategy, M&A advisory?
3. **Revenue split** -- On SaaS subscriptions? On consulting referrals?
4. **Development costs** -- Who pays for ongoing platform development?
5. **Data rights** -- Who owns the accumulated market intelligence data?
6. **Exclusivity duration** -- How long does Michiel have exclusive commercial rights?
7. **Performance thresholds** -- What happens if commercial targets are missed?
8. **Exit provisions** -- What happens if one party wants out?
9. **Non-compete** -- Can Eneve build or invest in a competing platform?
10. **John's role** -- Contractor? Employee? Advisor?
