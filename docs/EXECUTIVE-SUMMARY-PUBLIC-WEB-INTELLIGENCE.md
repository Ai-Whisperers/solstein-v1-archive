# Executive Summary: Public-Web Intelligence Expansion

> **Solstein Competitive Intelligence Platform**  
> **Prepared for:** Stakeholder Review  
> **Date:** March 7, 2026  
> **Read Time:** 2 minutes

---

## 🎯 The Opportunity

Transform Solstein from a research tool into a **complete public-web intelligence platform** that researches companies and markets as thoroughly as a team of analysts—but at scale, continuously, and with full evidence provenance.

**Current Gap:** We can search and enrich companies, but we don't yet:
- Crawl entire company websites deeply
- Track every claim back to its source
- Monitor public-web changes continuously
- Build complete market universes automatically
- Research from interviews, podcasts, and transcripts

**The Prize:** Investment-grade intelligence with complete audit trails, competitive coverage, and analyst workflow integration.

---

## 📊 What We Researched

Analyzed **300+ open-source repositories** across:
- Web crawling & document extraction
- Knowledge graphs & entity resolution
- LLM-powered research agents
- 100+ data source connectors
- Change detection & monitoring

**Key Finding:** The technology exists. The opportunity is to compose proven tools into a cohesive platform.

## 🗓️ The Plan: 33 Weeks, 4 Phases

| Phase | Duration | Focus | Deliverable |
|-------|----------|-------|-------------|
| **1. Foundation** | Weeks 1-8 | Evidence system + deep crawling | Every fact traceable to source |
| **2. Expansion** | Weeks 9-16 | Open data + entity resolution | 8+ source families, market universes |
| **3. Operations** | Weeks 17-24 | Monitoring + analyst workflow | Continuous intelligence, IC memos |
| **4. Global/Media** | Weeks 25-33 | Multilingual + transcripts | Global coverage, media intelligence |

**Quick Win:** Tangible evidence-grade capability in **first 4 weeks**.

## ⚠️ Key Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Crawling blocked by websites | Respect robots.txt, rate limits, use proxy rotation |
| Entity resolution errors | Confidence thresholds + human review workflow |
| Scope creep | Strict 4-week milestones, MVP-first approach |
| Integration complexity | Use standard interfaces (MCP, LangChain) |
