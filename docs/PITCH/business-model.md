## The Positioning Principle

Solstein is not a dashboard. It is not a data subscription. It is not a consulting engagement.

It is **capital infrastructure** — the intelligence layer that makes every acquisition decision, every portfolio review, and every exit preparation sharper than anything that existed before it.

Pricing reflects that. We are not competing with EUR 20K/yr data platforms. We are replacing EUR 1.5M consulting engagements. The price signal matters — cheap tools whisper uncertainty. Solstein speaks with authority.

## The Go-To-Market Incantation: Four Phases

The market is won in sequence. Each phase creates the foundation for the next.

```
Validate  →  Monetize  →  Authority  →  Scale  →  (Exit?)
```

### Phase 1 — The First Proving *(Month 1)*

One anchor client. One market. Full spell cast.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Target | 1 anchor PE firm (e.g., a mid-market European fund) |
| Commercial structure | EUR 60K pilot + success bonus tied to validated accuracy |
| Deliverable | Complete Attractiveness Board, 20–30 companies, all scores explained |
| Strategic outcome | Validated proof that Solstein scores predict real deal outcomes |

The success bonus is not a marketing tactic. It is a structural statement: **we believe our spell enough to eat the risk of its accuracy.**

### Phase 2 — Controlled Expansion *(Month 2)*

Three to five PE funds. Subscription relationships. The methodology proven once, now proven repeatedly.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Target | 3–5 PE funds |
| Commercial structure | Subscription offers with private demos from anchor case study |
| Revenue goal | EUR 180K–300K ARR |
| Strategic outcome | Clear ideal client profile, pricing validated, go-to-market engine running |

### Phase 3 — The Index *(Month 3–4)*

When Solstein publishes a controlled "European SaaS Attractiveness Index," it stops being a product and becomes **a market reference**. PE firms that are not subscribers start asking why they are not on the list.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Target | 20+ PE firms + Corporate Strategy teams |
| Commercial structure | Subscription + controlled publication of market indices |
| Revenue goal | 20× EUR 120K+ ARR contracted |
| Strategic outcome | Brand legitimacy, inbound interest, category ownership begins |

### Phase 4 — Structured Scale *(Month 5–8)*

The intelligence factory runs. Outbound becomes inbound. Roundtables create cross-selling. The market associates capital intelligence with Solstein.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Target | 100+ PE firms + Corp Dev teams |

Solstein's intelligence compounds:

| Source of Deepening | How It Works |

| **Deal validation feedback** | When PE clients tell us whether Solstein's classifications matched their actual outcomes, the scoring algorithm learns |

After 12 months of active use, a Solstein dataset is worth more than the dataset from the day it launched. The spell improves with casting.
