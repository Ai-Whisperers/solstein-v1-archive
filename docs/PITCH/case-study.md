## Analysis Outcomes | Structural Signal Mapping

### The Attractiveness Board

Every company was scored across three dimensions:

| Dimension | What Solstein Read |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Growth Score (0–10)** | Revenue trajectory, growth rate, margin health and direction |
| **Financial Health Score (0–10)** | Revenue scale against sector baseline, funding cushion, operational efficiency |
| **Competitive Position Score (0–10)** | AI adoption maturity, SaaS transition progress, tech stack depth, protocol certifications, geographic footprint |

### Structural Indicators | Deep-Dive Insights

- **15+ European energy protocols** mapped to exactly which companies hold certifications — exposing gaps competitors do not advertise
- **M&A genealogy** for 8 major players — full acquisition family trees showing who absorbed what and which synergies were claimed vs. realized
- **Country-by-country presence matrix** for all 29 companies — not headquarters, but actual operational presence
- **AI adoption signal vs. marketing claim** — companies that say "AI-powered" in their pitch vs. companies whose engineering teams, products, and hiring patterns confirm actual AI integration
