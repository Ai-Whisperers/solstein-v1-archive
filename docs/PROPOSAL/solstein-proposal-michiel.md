# Solstein Proposal

**AI-Driven Market Intelligence for Capital Allocation**

An adaptive market intelligence engine that identifies and ranks the true value drivers and players of each market.

Solstein is not an AI-scorecard. AI is the methodology. Capital efficiency is the outcome.

*(Also for markets where high AI-scores drive the value)*

---

## Product Concept

Solstein is an AI-orchestrated intelligence platform that a.o.:

- Maps markets continuously
- Identifies and ranks companies
- Evaluates growth, ownership, funding, profitability
- Measures adoption of key-drivers through output-based signals
- Assesses maturity and scalability
- Connects structural market drivers to company performance

The core output is an **Attractiveness Board**:

- Ranked and comparable
- Clickable per metric
- Fully explainable

When a criterion is selected, Solstein exposes:

- Underlying data
- Source signals
- Weighting logic (partially abstracted)
- AI reasoning path

This explainability layer is essential.
It prevents black-box risk and increases trust with PE and boards.

---

## Business Positioning

Solstein should be positioned from day one as a **standalone AI-native decision intelligence company**.

- Not as a feature.
- Not as an Eneve tool.
- Not as consulting support.

It requires its own go-to-market motion, commercial discipline, pricing structure and brand authority.

## Go-to-Market Architecture

Below is a structured launch routine where each phase follows the same pattern:

**Validate -> Monetize -> Authority -> Scale -> (Sale?)**

| Phase | Objective | Target | Commercial Action | Revenue Goal | Strategic Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1** -- Anchor Validation (Month 1) | Prove decision accuracy vs real deals | 1 Anchor PE (e.g., Vortex) | Paid pilot (discounted but structured) | EUR 60k pilot + success bonus | Validated scoring vs real acquisition outcomes |

| **Phase 3** -- Authority & Market Signal (Month 3-4) | Establish Solstein as an index (start) | 20 PE + Corporate Strategy | Publish controlled "European SaaS, market X, Attractiveness Index" | 20x EUR 120k+ ARR contracted | Brand legitimacy, inbound interest |
| **Phase 4** -- Structured Scale (Month 5-8) | Build repeatable sales engine | 100+ Wider PE + Corp Dev | Direct outbound + curated roundtables | EUR 10M ARR target | Market category ownership positioning |

## Commercial Model

**Initial focus: Private Equity**

Why:

- Highest urgency
- Direct ROI logic
- Repeat usage
- Clear budget holder

Pricing should signal strategic value. Solstein is capital infrastructure, not a dashboard tool:

- **PE subscription:** EUR 75k-150k per year
- Additional market modules priced separately
- Optional deep-dive deal reports

Avoid low-tier SaaS pricing.

---

## Organisational Model

Solstein should operate as:

- Separate legal entity (even if initially owned by Eneve)
- Lean team (AI lead, product lead, commercial lead)
- Clear KPI: ARR growth within 6-8 months

Continuous retraining of agents ensures:

- New rubrics are integrated
- Market manipulation signals are detected
- Scoring evolves dynamically

Defensibility grows through:

- Dataset accumulation
- Real deal validation feedback
- Proprietary weighting evolution

**Key metric in first 4 months:**

- EUR 1M contracted ARR target
- 5-8 high-ticket PE clients

## Strategic Narrative

### The Sunstone for Capital Navigation

In Viking navigation, the *solarsteinn* (the sunstone) allowed sailors to determine direction even when the sun was hidden behind clouds.

It did not replace the horizon.
It revealed it.

Solstein serves the same function in modern capital markets.

It turns market scanning into **continuous AI-driven capital intelligence** that detects structural value creation patterns, allowing markets to (not) price them in.
