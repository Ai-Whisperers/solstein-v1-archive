## Product Offering

tem operates as an energy marketplace/transaction platform:

- **Rosso** (AI Transaction Engine): ML algorithms and LLMs to predict supply and demand, optimize matching between generators and consumers, eliminate middlemen and margin stacking
- **RED** (Retail Energy Product): "First modern utility powered by AI-native infrastructure" -- transparent line-by-line billing based on half-hourly meter data (not monthly averages), claims 30% cheaper than traditional market rates
- **Generator Platform**: Direct connection for renewable generators to sell power to businesses
- **Business Platform**: Energy procurement for businesses with transparent pricing

Key metrics:
- 2,600+ business customers in UK
- Notable customers: Boohoo Group, Fever-Tree, Newcastle United FC
- Focus on renewable energy generators and small businesses

## Notes

- tem is NOT a direct competitor to Eneve in the traditional sense -- they operate at the retail/marketplace layer, not the wholesale back-office layer
- **However, tem matters because**:
  1. It demonstrates that AI-native energy platforms can attract $300M+ valuations
  2. Half-hourly meter data processing is core to both tem and Eneve
  3. If tem moves upstream into wholesale/balancing, their AI capabilities would be formidable
  4. The "Stripe of energy" API platform model could eventually encompass back-office functions
- tem represents where the energy software market is heading: AI-native, API-first, data-driven
- The funding gap is staggering: tem raised $75M in a single round; Engrate raised EUR 3M total
- Watch for: upstream expansion into wholesale, TSO/grid integration, European market entry
- tem's success validates the thesis that legacy energy software is ripe for AI-native disruption
