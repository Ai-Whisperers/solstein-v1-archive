## Product Offering

SOPTIM Elements is a modular cloud-based SaaS platform structured in three tiers:

- **Editionen (Editions)**: Compact all-in-one solutions for specific market roles (supplier, grid operator, balancing group manager)
- **Themengebiete (Thematic Areas)**: Solutions for selected topics (balancing, scheduling, market communication)
- **Elemente (Elements)**: Custom-fit applications for specific process steps

Key capabilities:
- **Balancing Group Management**: Automated processing with ALOCAT data, MaBiS file handling
- **Gas Balancing**: Daily nominations support, gas balancing group management
- **Scheduling & Nominations**: TSO schedule submission and management
- **Market Communication**: AS4 protocol integration, APERAK message confirmations
- **REMIT II Compliance**: Regulatory reporting for 2026 requirements
- **TSO Integration**: Serves 100% of German TSOs (long-standing Amprion partnership since 1975)

## Why We Missed This Competitor

SOPTIM was invisible in initial research because:
1. They don't position as "ETRM" -- they position as energy operations/back-office software
2. They sit in exactly the same niche as Eneve: balancing, scheduling, nominations, TSO communication
3. Search keywords "ETRM", "trading", "risk management" don't match their positioning
4. German-language primary website reduces English search visibility
