## Product Offering

KISTERS has been developing energy software since the deregulation of European energy markets in 1999. The BelVis product family provides:

- **BelVis Time Series Management**: Archiving, analyzing, and processing time series data for energy trading and accounting
- **BelVis+ PFM**: New cloud-based portfolio management and trading platform for European energy trading (official launch June 2026)
- **Energy Procurement & Trading**: Fully integrated IT solutions for end-user energy management
- **Retail & Distribution**: Retail energy management and distribution software
- **Grid & Metering**: Solutions for grid operations and metering data
- **Environmental Monitoring**: Water, air quality, and environmental data management (non-competing)

750+ energy companies rely on KISTERS energy management software and control systems.
