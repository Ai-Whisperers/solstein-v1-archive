- **Tier**: 2 - Broader ETRM with Back-Office (entering EU market)

- **Commodities**: Power, Gas, Oil, Renewables, Carbon, Crypto/Data Centers (50+ commodities)
- **Platform**: Cloud-native, API-first
- **Cloud / On-Premise**: Cloud-only SaaS

## Product Offering

Molecule's cloud-native ETRM/CTRM platform includes:

- **Core Platform**: Automated trade capture, real-time market data, advanced reporting, 30+ integrations
- **Elektra**: Power trading module (won 2025 Power Technology Excellence Award)
- **Hive**: Renewables trading and management (won 2025 Power Technology Excellence Award)
- **Djinn**: Crypto and data center energy trading module
- **Settlement**: Reconciliation and settlement capabilities
- **Risk**: Position management, mark-to-market, P&L tracking

Key milestones:
- 5+ million trades captured
- 25,000+ physical and financial products
- $100 billion+ daily commodity value
