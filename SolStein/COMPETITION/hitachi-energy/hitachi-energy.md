## Product Offering

Hitachi Energy's Energy Portfolio Management (EPM) division offers a suite of ETRM products, anchored by the TRMTracker platform acquired from Pioneer Solutions (2003, Denver CO) in September 2020:

- **TRMTracker**: Core C/ETRM platform providing front-to-back office functionality (trade capture, contract management, pricing, portfolio management, valuations, risk controls, credit management, settlement, regulatory compliance). Template-based, formula-driven architecture requiring no coding. Web-based, multi-commodity. Ranked #1 in 28 categories by Energy Risk Software Survey (2021)
- **RiskTracker**: Advanced risk analytics module with VaR, CFaR, EaR, PFE, CVA metrics
- **FASTracker**: Derivative accounting and hedge accounting compliance (FAS, IAS, ASC, IFRS). Automated prospective/retrospective effectiveness testing and journal entry generation
- **Market Operations**: Trade lifecycle management from deal capture through settlement
- **Portfolio Management**: Real-time portfolio evaluation and optimization
- **Regulatory Compliance**: EMIR, REMIT reporting with real-time audit trails
- **Grid Integration**: Unique advantage from Hitachi Energy's broader grid technology portfolio (HVDC, transformers, grid automation)
