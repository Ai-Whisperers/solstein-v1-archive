## Scoring Methodology

Each competitor pair is scored across **4 dimensions**, each contributing 0 or 1:

| Dimension | Scores 1 if... |
|---|---|
| **Geographic Overlap** | Both companies operate in at least 1 common market region (NL, DE, UK, Nordics, etc.) |
| **Product Overlap** | Both offer at least 1 common product category (Back-office, ETRM, Billing/CIS, etc.) |
| **Customer Segment Overlap** | Both target at least 1 common customer type (Retail utilities, Traders, Grid operators, etc.) |
| **Tier Proximity** | Companies are in the same or adjacent competitive tier (T1-T1b, T1b-T2, T2-T3) |

**Final score** = min(3, sum of dimensions). Score capped at 3: any pair overlapping on 3+ dimensions is a direct competitor.
