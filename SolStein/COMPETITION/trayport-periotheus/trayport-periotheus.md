## Product Offering

Periotheus is Trayport's energy management platform acquired in 2019. It provides:

- **Scheduling & Nominations**: Automated message exchange with TSOs, DSOs, and VTPs across European power and gas markets
- **Time Series Management**: Cross-market schedule format support
- **Message Processing**: Traffic-light status system for incoming/outgoing TSO communications, automatic ACK/ANO/CNF processing
- **Automation**: Fully automated operations with user interaction only on exceptions
- **REST API**: External integration for custom automation and programming
- **Market Coverage**: All common European schedule formats and markets

Trayport also operates a broader trading platform connecting 400+ clients for OTC energy trading, but Periotheus is the direct Eneve competitor.
