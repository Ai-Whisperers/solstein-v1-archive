# Solstein -- Pricing & Go-to-Market

---

## Pricing Tiers

### Tier 1: Single Portfolio Assessment

**EUR 500K - 1M**

- Full competitive landscape for one portfolio company
- Up to 30 competitors profiled (8 dimensions + financial scoring)
- Cross-competitor financial dashboard with rankings
- Corporate genealogy for top 10 competitors
- Market protocol/standard mapping
- Delivered in 1-2 weeks

**Ideal for**: PE firms evaluating a specific investment or preparing exit materials.

### Tier 2: Portfolio-Wide Intelligence

**EUR 2-3M** (one-time) + **EUR 300K/year** support

- Full Solstein deployment across up to 10 portfolio companies
- 150-300 competitor profiles total
- Portfolio-level competitive risk dashboard
- Quarterly refresh cycles included
- Industry vertical customization
- Dedicated support and prompt updates

**Ideal for**: PE/VC firms with 5-25 portfolio companies who want competitive intelligence as a permanent capability.

### Tier 3: Enterprise License

**EUR 3-5M** (one-time) + **EUR 500K/year**

- Unlimited portfolio companies
- Custom industry vertical development (3+ verticals)
- Full prompt architecture transfer (Solstein becomes an internal capability)
- Training for internal teams to operate and extend
- Priority support and co-development of new modules

**Ideal for**: Large PE firms or multi-fund managers who want to own the capability internally.

### Tier 4: SaaS Product (Future)

**EUR 100-200K/year per firm**

- Web-based interface
- Self-service competitor research
- Automated dashboard generation
- Multi-user access with role-based permissions
- API integration with portfolio management tools

**Revenue potential**: 100-500 PE/VC firms = EUR 10-100M ARR at scale.

## What Buyers Pay For

Not the text files. The expertise baked into them.

Anyone can ask an AI model to "research a competitor." The result is shallow, inconsistent, and unreliable. Solstein produces structured, scoreable, multi-dimensional intelligence with confidence-tagged data points -- because the person who built it spent 2 years learning exactly how to make AI do that.

## Go-to-Market

### Phase 1: Anchor Client (Now)

- Deploy Solstein for first PE firm (energy sector proven)
- Deliver full portfolio assessment as reference case
- Validate pricing and value proposition
- Collect testimonials and case study data

### Phase 2: Warm Network (3-6 months)

- Expand to 3-5 PE/VC firms through existing relationships
- Prioritize firms with portfolio companies in regulated industries (energy, finance, healthcare)
- Each engagement generates a reference case for the next
- Revenue target: EUR 5-15M

### Phase 3: Industry Verticals (6-12 months)

- Adapt Solstein to 3 additional industry verticals beyond energy
- Banking & FinTech (regulations, protocols, AI disruption -- same pattern)
- Healthcare & MedTech (complex regulatory landscape, AI transformation)
- Industrial Software (manufacturing, logistics, supply chain)
- Revenue target: EUR 15-30M

### Phase 4: SaaS Platform (12-24 months)

- Productize into self-service SaaS platform
- Automated research, dashboard generation, refresh scheduling
- Subscription model for recurring revenue
- Target: 100+ PE/VC firms
- Revenue target: EUR 10-20M ARR
