# Solstein -- Case Study: European Energy Software Market

---

## The Engagement

Full competitive landscape analysis for a European energy software company preparing for PE exit positioning.

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Competitors profiled | **29** (across 4 tiers) |
| Research dimensions per company | **8 competitive + 6 financial** |
| Time to full landscape | **Days, not months** |
| Financial dashboard generated | **Automated from individual profiles** |
| Corporate genealogies documented | **Complete M&A family trees with diagrams** |
| Market protocols mapped | **5 protocol categories across multiple countries** |

---

## Depth Achieved

Each competitor profile includes:
- Financial growth scoring (1-10 across 6 dimensions)
- AI adoption assessment (None / Low / Moderate / Strong / Very Strong)
- SaaS maturity rating (1-10)
- Threat level classification (Low through Critical)
- Corporate genealogy with full M&A history
- Confidence tags on every data point

---

## Intelligence Gap Closed

The engagement revealed:
- **4 competitors** the client had never heard of
- **2 competitors** on the client's watchlist were less threatening than assumed
- **1 competitor** that was invisible turned out to run critical national infrastructure
- The client's own **AI maturity ranking**: not even on the board

This intelligence directly informed board-level strategy and M&A positioning.
