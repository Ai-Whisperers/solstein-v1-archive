## Competitive Profile

- **Tier**: 2 - Broader ETRM
- **Specialization**: Full front-to-back commodity trading and risk management, multi-commodity
- **Commodities**: Power, Gas, Renewables, Liquids, Carbon Certificates, Agricultural
- **Platform**: Enterprise-grade, integrated analytics
- **Cloud / On-Premise**: Both (historically on-premise, adding cloud capabilities)

## Product Offering

Allegro is an enterprise-scale CTRM platform providing:

- **Trade Capture**: Multi-commodity deal capture and management
- **Risk Management**: Integrated advanced analytics, real-time risk assessment
- **Settlement & Accounting**: Back-office settlement, invoicing, accounting integration
- **Logistics**: Physical delivery and supply chain management
- **Regulatory Compliance**: EMIR, REMIT, Dodd-Frank reporting
- **Market Data**: Integration with market data providers
- **Portfolio Management**: Cross-commodity portfolio optimization

Endur (also ION) provides similar capabilities with different architecture, often seen in the same competitive evaluations.
