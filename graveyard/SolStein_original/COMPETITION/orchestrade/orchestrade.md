## Product Offering

Orchestrade provides an integrated front-to-back ETRM platform:

- **Front Office**: Trade capture, deal management, cross-border trading
- **Middle Office**: Real-time risk calculations, position management, P&L
- **Back Office**: Settlement management (monthly/final settlement), caps/floors, negative price handling
- **Power Trading**: Capacity scheduling, generation asset management, renewable PPA management
- **Environmental**: Carbon, REC, and environmental certificate trading
- **Regulatory**: EMIR, REMIT, MiFID II reporting

Key differentiator: deployment speed -- BB Energy fully deployed in under 10 weeks (October 2025), compared to industry-standard 4.5-month implementations.
