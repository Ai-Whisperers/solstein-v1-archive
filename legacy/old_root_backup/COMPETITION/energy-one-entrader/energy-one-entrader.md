## Product Offering

Energy One provides trading software and operational services:

- **enTrader ETRM**: Multi-commodity, multi-user trading with real-time portfolio evaluation (market risk, credit risk, P&L, cash flows)
- **Scheduling, Nominations & Bidding**: Automated TSO communication and market bidding
- **enPrice**: Market pricing and forecasting
- **enVoy**: Market connectivity and messaging
- **Retail Portfolio Management**: Energy supplier portfolio management module
- **Regulatory Compliance**: EMIR, REMIT compliance
- **24/7 Operational Services**: Outsourced energy operations - largest in Australia, second largest in Europe
- **"Follow-the-Sun" Model**: Teams across Australia and Europe provide continuous coverage

450+ customer installations across 30+ countries, 2,000+ users globally.
